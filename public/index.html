ACT III • THE FOUR ARENAS

Four Ways We Bridge the Gap

Every round challenges players with dynamic situations drawn from real teen and young adult experiences.

01 • IN THEIR SHOES 👟

Perspective Taking & Real Support

Step directly into someone else's dilemma—a classmate folding over in silent period cramps, or a friend lashing out when overwhelmed. First, decode what they truly feel. Then, choose the kindest, most supportive move.

Mastered Skill: Emotional resonance and active support.
02 • REAL OR REEL? 📱

Media Literacy & Science Fact-Checking

Viral TikToks claim chocolate causes cramps, vasectomies are irreversible, or crying weakens you. Separate medical reality from social media myths backed by verified health sources.

Mastered Skill: Critical thinking and scientific literacy.
03 • SAY IT SMOOTH 💬

Compassionate Boundaries & Consent

Master the delicate balance between honesty and empathy. Practice how to set a healthy personal boundary, navigate awkward crush moments, or communicate consent without ghosting or hurting others.

Mastered Skill: Assertive, compassionate communication.
04 • CAPTION THIS! ✍️

De-escalating Stress Through Shared Humor

Transform everyday awkward chaos—from midnight group project texts to clumsy social moments—into creative, funny, or heartfelt captions. Vote anonymously on your favorites.

Mastered Skill: Finding warmth and connection through humor.
