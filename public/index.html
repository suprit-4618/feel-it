FEEL IT

The party game about stepping into someone else’s shoes.

A real-time multiplayer game for 2 to 8 players that tests emotional intelligence, busts social media myths, and rewards thoughtful communication.

OVERVIEW

How Feel It Works

Connect from any phone or browser using a 4-letter room code. Compete across 10 fast-paced rounds while collaborating to fill the shared Team Empathy Meter.

GAME MODES

Four Ways to Score

01

Step Into Their Shoes

Analyze relatable interpersonal dilemmas. Identify how someone actually feels, then choose the kindest and most supportive response.

02

Real or Reel?

Spot viral misinformation. Distinguish fact from fiction across health, relationships, social trends, and wellness advice.

03

Say It Smooth

Find the line that balances honesty with respect. Practice confident, clear communication in awkward social situations.

04

Caption This

Submit funny or insightful captions for everyday scenarios, then vote anonymously on the best contributions from the room.

COOPERATIVE MECHANIC

The Team Empathy Meter

Individual scores determine the podium, but teamwork matters. When the room consistently chooses constructive, empathetic answers, the shared meter fills. Reaching 100% rewards every player with a +200 point endgame bonus.

BUILT FOR EVERYONE

Zero Logins. No Tracking.

  • No Passwords: Jump straight in with a nickname and room code.
  • Mobile First: Optimized for mobile screens and touch interactions.
  • Content Moderation: Built-in language filtering and host controls.
